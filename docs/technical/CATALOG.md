## How to Write a Goal

Type your goal in the **Buyer Agent** input box. The AI understands natural language.


**Format that works best:**
```
buy <product description> under <budget> rupees
buy <product description> for less than <budget> INR
find me <product description> within <budget>
purchase <product description> below <budget>
```

**Tips:**
- Always include a budget — it sets the token's `maxAmount`
- Be descriptive but not exact — the AI maps descriptions to SKUs
- The AI will `HALT` if no catalog item matches your goal
